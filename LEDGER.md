# Commercial Income Ledger

Every fee, investment and contribution this project receives, and every payment it makes, is recorded here: the date,
the amount, who paid (with their agreement to be named), and what the money was spent on, with the matching receipts
or invoices. Nothing is left out and nothing is hidden. The money is used only for the development of this project. See the
pledge in the [README](README.md#every-commercial-payment-goes-back-into-the-project).
