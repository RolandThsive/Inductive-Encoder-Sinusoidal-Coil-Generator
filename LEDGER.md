# Commercial Income Ledger

Every fee, investment and contribution this project receives, and every payment it makes, is recorded here: the date,
the amount, who paid (the name may be struck out to protect confidentiality, but never the receipt or the transaction details), and what the money was spent on, with the matching receipts
or invoices. Nothing is left out and nothing is hidden. The money is used only for the equipment and services used to build this project. Even a single cent is recorded here. See the
pledge in the [README](README.md#every-commercial-payment-goes-back-into-the-project).
