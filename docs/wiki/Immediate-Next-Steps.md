# 30. Immediate Next Steps

- **Hardware Testing.** Funds are being collected to order a wide range of coil PCBs and the test
  equipment to measure them.
- **Model Validation.** Every measured board will be compared with the analyser's predictions, and
  the models will be corrected wherever they disagree.
- **Wider Coverage.** Test boards will cover different loop counts, sub-coils, TX layouts and rotor
  shapes, so that the project becomes one of the most capable inductive rotary sensor coil
  generators available.
- **Dual Generator Boards.** Dual boards (a coarse and a fine track on one stator) will be built and measured too,
  including whether two free-running TX oscillators work side by side, which has not been shown yet.

The order of the work is set by the milestones in
[`MILESTONES.md`](https://github.com/RolandThsive/Inductive-Encoder-Sinusoidal-Coil-Generator/blob/main/MILESTONES.md):
the generators first (single, dual, arc and linear), then the testing of each, the corrections from the measurements,
and working examples.

If you can help with boards, equipment or measurements, please see [Licensing](License.md) for
contact details.
